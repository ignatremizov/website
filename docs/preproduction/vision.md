# Portfolio World Vision

## Purpose

Build an explorable portfolio that makes Ignat Remizov's systems work and creative
range memorable without making visitors work to find basic professional information.

## Audience

- Hiring managers, founders, technical leaders, and collaborators.
- Engineers curious about the implementation.
- Friends and internet wanderers who want to discover the playful material.
- Mobile visitors who may only have a minute.

## Design pillars

1. **Readable systems.** Every route, interaction, and interface should make its
   purpose visible.
2. **Strange skies, human scale.** The world may be magical or surreal, while the
   player, writing, and portfolio remain warm and grounded.
3. **Reward curiosity.** Essential content is direct; exploration reveals extra
   context, jokes, old-site artifacts, and cheats.

## Writing voice

Public-facing copy should sound direct, specific, and human. Avoid em dashes and
formulaic contrast constructions. Use ordinary sentence structure, commas, colons,
parentheses, or separate sentences instead. Date ranges use en dashes. Theatrical
NPC dialogue may use deliberate rhetorical devices when they suit a distinct
character voice.

Discovery is allowed to stay mysterious. Outside NPC dialogue and an optional future
guide, inspecting a world object should report a sensory detail or in-world
observation. It should not explain the mechanic, prerequisite, or next action.
Pictorial clues, animation, light, sound, and consequences carry that information
through play.

## Experience promise

The first screen identifies Ignat, his role, and the two available paths: explore the
world or open the complete Quick Portfolio. The playable route begins in the Field
Station and uses portals to reach the Luminous Falls, Signal Grid, and secret Space
Archive.

## Vertical-slice behavior promise

- Portal surfaces and their glyphs are translucent thresholds that remain legible
  from either face.
- Spell charge is both visible and consequential: holding longer increases digit
  count, size, spread, and cast power. Surge reads as an especially dense violet/cyan
  storm before the player earns Thunder.
- The planning-table compass is a collectible rather than ambient dressing. Picking
  it up clips the physical prop onto the avatar's hip and unlocks a live HUD heading
  for the current expedition. The compass remains directly draggable from that clip;
  removing it hides the heading until it is collected again. Respawn can recover a
  loose compass without granting ownership.
- The void turtle's automatic bounce belongs only at the void edge, never on safe
  ground. It appears asleep before Thunder, while the lantern can uncover a nearby
  lightning-marked stone without explanatory UI copy. Once Thunder is available, an
  intentional interaction starts its ride.
- Inhabited spaces show visible moving NPCs and fish. Opening the hidden chest
  answers with a celebratory burst of coins and light and evolves Spark into Surge.
  The earlier waterfall ritual opens the route to the chest.
- The Falls should read as a living vertical place: quality-bounded vegetation frames
  a discoverable ledge route across its moss terraces and waterfall crowns.
- Pong and Archive are independent challenges that may resolve in either order. Once
  both are complete and Surge is available, the spell evolves into Thunder.

## Non-goals for the vertical slice

- A finished open world.
- Production Blender assets.
- Multiplayer or server persistence.
- A complete combat, inventory, or quest system.
- Physically simulated fluids or a general sandbox physics engine.

## Success

- A visitor understands the person and offer before entering the game.
- Movement and interaction feel good on keyboard and touch.
- Every important destination can be reached by walking, the map, or the terminal.
- The first playable load remains within the agreed performance budget.
- The site remains useful when WebGL, motion, or game controls are unavailable.
